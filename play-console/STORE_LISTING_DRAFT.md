## Full description

CardScanR helps Pokémon TCG collectors scan cards, build a collection, and view approximate market values.

**Features**
- Camera scanning with on-device text recognition  
- Manual search and match review when OCR is uncertain  
- Local collection storage with optional cloud sync when signed in  
- Binders and collection goals  
- Estimate pricing from available catalogue and market data sources  
- Google or email sign-in  

**Honest limitations**
- Recognition can misread variants, languages, glare, or damaged cards — review matches before saving  
- Values are estimates only, not guarantees of what a card will sell for  
- Marketplace availability and sold prices change continuously  
- Some pricing providers may be unavailable or not configured in a given build  

CardScanR is an independent app and is not affiliated with Nintendo, Creatures, Game Freak, The Pokémon Company, eBay, or third-party catalogue providers.

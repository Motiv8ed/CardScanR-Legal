Account deletion

Delete Account and Data — CardScanR: TCG Card Scanner

Package: com.cardscanr.app · CardScanR is operated by Andrew Gore.

Two ways to start a deletion request

You can initiate account deletion inside CardScanR through Settings → Privacy & Security → Request account deletion. That in-app control opens this hosted deletion request page; it does not complete deletion by itself. You may also submit the request through this page or by emailing cardscanr.dev@gmail.com.

1. Identify the app

This deletion process applies to CardScanR: TCG Card Scanner, Android package com.cardscanr.app.

2. Initiate deletion inside CardScanR

  1. Open CardScanR
  2. Go to Settings → Data & privacy → Privacy & Security
  3. Open the section Delete account and cloud data
  4. Select Request account deletion
  5. Complete the request on this hosted page (or use the email instructions below)

The in-app control only opens this hosted request process. It does not wipe your account immediately inside the app.

Settings → Account offers Sign out only. Sign out does not delete your CardScanR account and does not remove local collection data from the device.

3. Submit a request on this page or by email

Email cardscanr.dev@gmail.com to request deletion of your CardScanR account and associated cloud collection data. You do not need to sign in to this website.

Email a deletion request Support page

A static HTML form without a working backend is intentionally not provided. Requesting deletion does not automatically erase your account; verified requests are processed by the operator.

4. What to include

  • The email address on your CardScanR account
  • Whether you signed in with Google or email/password
  • The phrase “CardScanR account deletion request”
  • Any other detail needed to locate the account

Do not email passwords, payment card numbers, or one-time codes.

5. Identity verification

We may ask you to confirm control of the account email (for example by replying from that address or completing a confirmation step). We will not process deletion requests we cannot reasonably verify.

6. What the request covers

Subject to verification, a deletion request is intended to cover the CardScanR account and associated cloud collection data, including where present:

  • Authentication account
  • Profile
  • Customer collection items
  • Binders
  • Binder memberships
  • Sync preferences
  • Sync operations and checkpoints
  • Beta feedback or beta-program records where associated with the account

7. Local device data

Local device data is separate from cloud account data. Inventory, caches, and preferences may remain on the device unless you remove them through the app or device (for example by clearing app storage or uninstalling CardScanR). Sign-out does not wipe local collection data.

  • Backups and logs held by infrastructure providers may persist for a limited operational period.
  • Legal holds if retention is required by law.

8. Processing

Verified deletion requests are processed by the operator after identity confirmation. Completion timing depends on verification and operational review; this page does not promise a fixed completion timeframe or retention period.

9. Irreversible action

Warning.

Account and cloud data deletion may be irreversible. Export any collection data you need before requesting deletion. CSV export is not currently implemented in the app.

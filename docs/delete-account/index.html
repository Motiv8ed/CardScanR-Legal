Account deletion

Delete Account and Data — CardScanR: TCG Card Scanner

Package: com.cardscanr.app · CardScanR is operated by Andrew Gore.

In-app deletion is not available yet.

The current CardScanR build does not provide an in-app account-deletion control. The Privacy and trust screen states that a delete-account button is not shown until deletion is implemented end to end. Until that ships, use the email request method below.

1. Identify the app

This deletion process applies to CardScanR: TCG Card Scanner, Android package com.cardscanr.app.

2. In-app path (current status)

Verified path today:

  1. Open CardScanR
  2. Go to Settings → Data & privacy → Privacy and trust
  3. Note the message that in-app account deletion is not available in this build
  4. Settings → Account offers Sign out only. Sign-out keeps local collection data on the device and does not delete your cloud account

3. External deletion request

Email cardscanr.dev@gmail.com to request deletion of your CardScanR account and associated cloud data.

Email a deletion request Support page

A static HTML form without a working backend is intentionally not provided.

4. What to include

  • The email address on your CardScanR account
  • Whether you signed in with Google or email/password
  • The phrase “CardScanR account deletion request”
  • Any other detail needed to locate the account

Do not email passwords, payment card numbers, or one-time codes.

5. Identity verification

We may ask you to confirm control of the account email (for example by replying from that address or completing a confirmation step). We will not process deletion requests we cannot reasonably verify.

6. What is deleted

Subject to verification, a deletion request is intended to cover cloud account data associated with CardScanR, including where present:

  • Your authentication account for the app
  • Cloud profile data
  • Cloud collection and card records for that account
  • Cloud scan-session records for that account

7. What may remain

  • Local device data (inventory, caches, preferences) remains until you clear app storage or uninstall. Sign-out does not wipe local collection data.
  • Backups and logs held by infrastructure providers may persist for a limited operational period.
  • Legal holds if retention is required by law.

8. Processing time

Verified deletion requests will normally be completed within 30 days.

9. Irreversible action

Warning.

Account and cloud data deletion may be irreversible. Export any collection data you need before requesting deletion. CSV export is not currently implemented in the app.

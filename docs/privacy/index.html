Privacy

Privacy Policy for CardScanR: TCG Card Scanner

Effective: 27 July 2026 · Last updated: 27 July 2026
App: CardScanR: TCG Card Scanner · Package: com.cardscanr.app

1. Who operates CardScanR

CardScanR is operated by Andrew Gore.

Contact for privacy and support: cardscanr.dev@gmail.com.

2. Scope

This policy describes information processed by the CardScanR Android application and related backend services used by the app. It does not claim compliance with any particular privacy law as a blanket guarantee. Where you have rights under applicable law, we will respond to valid requests through the contact method above.

3. Information you provide

Account and sign-in

CardScanR uses Supabase Authentication. You may sign in with:

  • Google account (OAuth) via Supabase’s Google provider. Google and Supabase process authentication data under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Email and password registered through Supabase Auth. Email address and authentication credentials are processed by Supabase.

From the signed-in account, the app may read an email address and display name / full name from auth metadata for profile display and account screens.

Profile and onboarding

When signed in, the app stores a profile in the cloud (including items such as display name, country code, currency code, pricing market, pricing mode, main use case, TCG interests, and onboarding completion status).

Collection, binders, and goals

Card inventory is stored in a local database on your device. When you are signed in, supported inventory and collection records may sync to the cloud. Synced card records can include card identity fields, condition, quantity, purchase and estimate fields, notes, tags, catalogue image URLs, and a local path or storage-location string field. Binder layouts and some planning data are described in-app as remaining on-device unless a signed-in sync path explicitly supports them.

Scan sessions

When signed in, the app can save scan session metadata to the cloud, which may include scan mode, source, status, counts, and raw OCR text. This is metadata about scanning activity, not a guarantee that full card photographs are uploaded.

4. Card scans, photographs, and OCR

CardScanR requests camera permission when you open scanning. You may also choose images from your gallery via the system picker. Frames and photos are used to identify card text and artwork.

On-device OCR: Text recognition uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition and OpenCV processing on the device.

Cloud Vision: A Google Cloud Vision OCR option is present in the product source only as an unimplemented stub and does not currently send images.

In-app privacy copy states that captured scan images stay on the device unless you deliberately export diagnostics or use a workflow that clearly sends evidence for processing. Separately, signed-in sync can upload card metadata (including catalogue image URLs and path strings). We do not claim that photographs are immediately deleted, encrypted at rest, anonymised, or retained for a fixed period.

5. Pricing and card-data requests

To match cards and show estimate values, the app may send card identity queries (such as name, set, collector number, language, or variant) to third-party or backend services, including:

  • TCGdex API
  • Pokémon TCG API
  • PokeWallet API when configured
  • An optional CardScanR pricing backend when configured at build time
  • Cloud catalogue and image manifest lookups for official card artwork references

An eBay sold-comps pricing module exists in source but is not configured with mobile API credentials in the current app. Pricing figures are estimates only.

6. Device, preference, and diagnostic information

  • Local preferences: On-device settings such as onboarding flags, developer-mode unlocks, market preferences, and UI options.
  • Local caches: Catalogue search indexes, artwork caches, and similar files may be stored on device.
  • Diagnostics: Optional troubleshooting exports can include app state, scan evidence, pricing status, and device-local logs. Developer diagnostics should remain off unless you are intentionally collecting a support bundle.
  • Crash and analytics SDKs: The current app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, or advertising SDKs.

7. Purposes for processing

  • Create and authenticate your account
  • Complete onboarding and apply country/currency/pricing preferences
  • Recognise cards and maintain your collection
  • Sync supported cloud inventory when signed in
  • Retrieve catalogue metadata and estimated market values
  • Provide optional diagnostic exports you choose to generate
  • Respond to support and deletion requests

8. Local versus cloud processing

Primarily local
Camera frames for live scanning, on-device OCR, local inventory storage, binder planning data (unless synced), preferences, and optional local diagnostic files.
Cloud / network
Authentication and database sync for signed-in users; HTTPS requests to catalogue and pricing providers; download of catalogue artwork and manifest data; OAuth redirects for Google sign-in.

9. Third-party service providers

Depending on features you use, providers may include Supabase (authentication and database), Google (sign-in and on-device ML Kit), TCGdex, Pokémon TCG API, PokeWallet, and any configured CardScanR pricing or image hosting backends. Each provider processes data under its own policies.

10. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared with service providers only as needed to operate CardScanR (authentication, sync, catalogue, and pricing lookups), or if required by law. Card identity queries sent to catalogue and pricing APIs are necessary to return match and price estimates.

11. International processing

Supabase and other providers may process data in data centres outside Australia.

12. Security

The app uses HTTPS for network calls and Supabase client keys intended for publishable client use. No security measure is perfect. Do not email passwords or payment secrets to support.

13. Retention

Local data remains on your device until you clear app storage, uninstall, or overwrite it. Cloud account, profile, collection, and scan-session data remain until deleted through a supported deletion process or otherwise removed by the operator.

14. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your CardScanR account and associated cloud data from a CardScanR build that includes the account-deletion request entry: open Settings → Data & privacy → Privacy & Security, then on the Privacy and trust screen select Request account deletion. That action opens the Delete Account and Data page. You can also request deletion by emailing cardscanr.dev@gmail.com without signing in to this website. Requests cover the account and associated cloud data subject to identity verification, and verified requests will normally be completed within 30 days. Sign out does not delete the account and does not remove local collection data; local data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall CardScanR. Deletion may be irreversible. Export anything you need before requesting deletion (CSV export is not currently implemented in the app).

15. Your choices and rights

  • Deny camera permission (manual entry remains available per in-app copy)
  • Avoid gallery selection if you do not want the app to read chosen photos
  • Sign out (local data remains)
  • Request account and cloud data deletion via Delete Account and Data or support email
  • Request access or correction of account information where applicable law provides that right

16. Children and target audience

You must have the legal capacity to agree to use CardScanR where you live. CardScanR is not directed to children.

17. Changes

We may update this policy as the app or providers change. The effective and last-updated dates above will be revised when a new version is published.

18. Contact

Privacy questions for CardScanR: TCG Card Scanner: cardscanr.dev@gmail.com.

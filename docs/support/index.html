Support

CardScanR Support

Help for CardScanR: TCG Card Scanner (com.cardscanr.app). This page explains common problems. To report a bug or suggest a feature, use the Feedback form.

Contact

Primary: Secure feedback form — best for bugs, incorrect data, and improvement ideas.

Secondary: Email cardscanr.dev@gmail.com for account, privacy, or sign-in help when the form is not suitable.

We aim to review beta feedback within 3–5 business days.

Report a bug or suggest an improvement Email support
Privacy warning.

Do not send passwords, payment card details, one-time codes, recovery keys, or government identifiers. If you export a diagnostics bundle, review it before sharing — diagnostics may include scan evidence, pricing status, and device-local logs.

Closed beta installation

  • Testers must be added using the Google account used by their Play Store.
  • After approval, open the Google Play opt-in link provided to you (not published here).
  • The Play listing may take time to appear after you join the beta programme.
  • If the wrong Play Store account opens, switch accounts in the Play Store app and try again.
  • Apply for closed beta access at Closed beta when applications are open.

Google sign-in

  • Confirm you are using the Play / release package com.cardscanr.app.
  • If you cancel login or pick the wrong Google account, start sign-in again from CardScanR and choose the correct account.
  • After Google sign-in in the browser, return to the app. If the app does not open, use the OAuth return page deep link cardscanr://login-callback or reopen CardScanR.
  • For email sign-in, check spam for verification mail and confirm the email address is typed correctly.
  • If the browser returns without completing sign-in, close the tab, reopen CardScanR, and try again. See Sign-in return.
  • Sign out in Settings does not delete your account or local collection data.
  • Contact support without sharing passwords or verification codes.

Scanner help

  • Grant camera permission when prompted — without it, live scanning is unavailable (manual entry may still work).
  • Use good lighting and reduce glare; tilt the card slightly if reflections block the collector number.
  • Fill the on-screen guide with the card face; move closer when the collector number is hard to read.
  • If autofocus hunts repeatedly, hold steady, tap to focus if your device supports it, or try gallery import.
  • Gallery import: choose a clear, flat photo of the card.
  • Review OCR text and match suggestions before saving — correct errors in the review step.

Collection and binder help

  • Missing card images: Catalogue artwork loads from reference URLs; check your connection and try again.
  • Incorrect card identity: Edit the entry or report via Feedback with card, set, and collector number.
  • Quantities and condition: Edit the item in your collection or binder view.
  • Binder completion / missing targets: Confirm the binder template and that items are assigned to the correct slot.
  • Offline / local data: Your inventory stays on-device by default; cloud sync is optional and opt-in.
  • Cloud sync: When enabled while signed in, collection metadata syncs — not camera photographs.

Pricing help

  • Displayed values are estimates, not offers to buy or sell and not financial advice.
  • The app shows the pricing source and freshness where available — stale prices may not reflect current market activity.
  • If a price looks wrong, note the card, set, source, and date shown, then report via Feedback.
  • Do not make purchase or sale decisions based solely on in-app estimates.
